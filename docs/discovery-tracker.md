## Written commitment minimum

A commitment counts only when a qualified team identifies an integration owner, a Fuji
transaction-building path, a start window, and permission to publish at least an
anonymous completion/failure result. Interest, a social-media reply, or attendance at a
demo does not count.

Suggested text:

> We intend to evaluate AVAX Impact on Fuji in `[transaction path]`. `[role/name]` owns
> the integration, with a target start in `[window]`. We understand attribution is a
> public, spoofable declaration and that failed or abandoned results will be recorded
> `[publicly/anonymously]`. This is not an endorsement or promise to deploy to mainnet.
