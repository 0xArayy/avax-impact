# Community conduct

Project spaces must remain useful and safe for technical collaboration. Be respectful,
assume good intent while checking claims, and criticize work without attacking people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disclosure of private
security reports are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns through the private channel described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); include only the information needed to investigate.
